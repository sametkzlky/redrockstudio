About

A studio that loves making games.

Redrock Studio is an independent game studio creating original games for mobile platforms. We handle design, development, art production and publishing entirely in-house.

Our goal is to make games that are fun from the first minute, respect players' time and budget, and stay worth playing for a long time. We keep improving every game after launch with regular updates and new content.

  • Player experience comes first If a mechanic doesn't feel good on the first try, we redesign it.
  • Fair and transparent We aim for balanced progression and clearly state ads and purchase options on our store pages.
  • Quality and consistency Performance, accessibility and regular content updates.
